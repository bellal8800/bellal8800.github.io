03 · DEMAND

For this simplified concentric case, net vertical shear is column load minus upward soil reaction enclosed by the perimeter.

Important: The displayed percentages mean (1) enclosed soil reaction as a percentage of Pᵤ and (2) simplified net demand as a percentage of Pᵤ. They are not percentages of punching shear assigned to different perimeter sides. Punching shear is checked at the critical perimeter; moment transfer, rectangular columns, edge/corner columns, footing geometry, self-weight, and the governing code can change the real calculation. This is an educational model, not a design approval.
