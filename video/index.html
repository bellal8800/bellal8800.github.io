What to notice: The column pushes down on the footing. The critical perimeter is the code-defined check boundary around the loaded area. Punching shear is checked along that boundary.

This is a conceptual CSS 3D model, not a finite-element simulation. Geometry and sliders are illustrative, not to scale.
